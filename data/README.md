# Carisurg-portfolio

A clinical AI research portfolio developed as part of the CariSurg MedTech Pathways Programme (Cohort 2026).

# What this is?

This repository contains the work produced during a 12-week clinical AI research programme hosted by CariSurg, in collaboration with Mercer General Hospital's Clinical AI & Innovation Unit. The programme focuses on developing and evaluating AI tools for real-world healthcare settings in the Caribbean.

The current project investigates an explainable AI support tool for high-risk patient identification during emergency department triage.

# Who this is for?

This portfolio is intended for clinical reviewers, programme supervisors, and collaborating researchers at Mercer General Hospital and the CariSurg cohort. No programming experience is required to read the documentation and proposals in `docs/`.
