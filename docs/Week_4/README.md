## CariSurg MedTech Pathways Programme — Cohort 2026

A 12-week clinical AI research programme run in collaboration with Mercer 
General Hospital's Clinical AI & Innovation Unit. The programme develops 
technical, ethical, and systems-thinking skills through weekly deliverables 
that build toward a full research proposal and pilot design.

### Project
**AI-Assisted Triage Support Tool for High-Risk Patient Identification 
in the Emergency Department**

The project proposes an explainable AI support tool for emergency department 
triage at Mercer General Hospital. The tool combines gradient boosting and 
natural language processing to flag high-risk patients for nurse review, 
displaying a human-readable reason for each alert. Nurses retain full 
accountability for all final escalation decisions.
